C8b wins the tournament. It satisfies the judge's criteria in order: a named app
(YawnScore) with a precisely useless function; funny through deadpan confidence
(a productivity metric for yawning, a badge for yawning in your own review);
a real-pitch shape (product name, function, audience: coworkers); 193 of 240
chars in one clean sentence with no padding and no second idea; and a vivid,
specific picture (the badge, pinned where your manager can see it). The
founder-certainty is in the tone: it never winks. The runner-up C12 is kept
in mind only if the finisher needs a replacement variant — but Force_Last
