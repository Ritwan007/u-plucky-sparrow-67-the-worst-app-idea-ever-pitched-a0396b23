One sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable, entered in the
[The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/c/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23)
competition on make.fun.

The entry program (`main.py`, Python 3.13) writes a single sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> YawnScore converts every yawn into a productivity metric, ranks your tiredness against your coworkers, and pins a badge to your company profile when you yawn during your own performance review.

YawnScore turns yawning into a productivity metric and pins the badge to
your company profile — a founder-certain pitch for a function nobody on
earth needs. Sixteen original candidates, scored against the judge's five
criteria with a pairwise tournament, are in `candidates.md`.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
